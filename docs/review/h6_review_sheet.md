# DRLCA legal review sheet (H6)

## A note for the reviewer

Thank you for agreeing to read this. **DRLCA** is a free website that answers questions about the rights of persons with disabilities in Nigeria. It answers only from four documents: the **Discrimination Against Persons with Disabilities (Prohibition) Act 2018**, the **1999 Constitution**, a **2020 Factsheet** that summarises the Act, and (new in this version of the sheet) the **Persons with Disabilities (Accessibility) Regulations, 2023** (S.I. No. 51 of 2023). It also points people to the DRAC helplines and to disability organisations.

*This sheet replaces the earlier three-document version you may have received (`h6_review_sheet_v2_2026-10-21`, kept unchanged). The questions and AI answers are the same; the excerpts now come from the four documents, and there are four new questions about the Regulations just below.*

**What every user sees.** For each question the site shows short passages ("excerpts") taken word for word from the four documents. Each excerpt starts with a citation tag that says where it came from:

* `[Act cl. N]` — clause N of the Disability Act 2018
* `[Constitution s. N]` — section N of the 1999 Constitution
* `[Factsheet Section N]` — Section N of the 2020 Factsheet
* `[Regs reg. N]` — regulation N of the Accessibility Regulations 2023 (`[Regs general]` is their unnumbered opening text: title, commencement date and enacting power)

These four numbering schemes are different: Act clause 12, Constitution section 12 and regulation 12 are not the same provision. The excerpt text is copied exactly as it appears in our copy of each document, including any scanning errors (odd characters, broken words), because we never alter the source text without a human check. The site shows the first three excerpts and folds the rest under a "more" link.

**A fixed note on the Regulations.** Whenever excerpts from the Regulations are shown, the site shows one fixed, hand-written note about their legal status ("About this source: ...") once, just before the first Regulations excerpt, and reads it aloud there. It is the same text every time and is never generated by the AI. It appears in this sheet exactly where a user would see it.

**The AI answer is optional.** A user can switch on an "AI answer" that writes a short plain-English answer from the excerpts, with a citation tag after each claim. It is **OFF by default**, so most users only ever see the excerpts. The AI answers in this sheet were recorded in September and October 2026 in our own tests. A refusal ("I can't answer this from the Disability Act 2018, the 1999 Constitution, or the factsheet I have...") is the intended behaviour when the documents do not answer the question. **The AI answers were recorded before the Regulations were added, and the AI does not yet use the Regulations excerpts** (the site says so next to the AI answer).
