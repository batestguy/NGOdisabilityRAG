be reproducible from an ARTIFACT rather than from prose in a journal entry, or

WHICH NUMBERS ARE COMPARABLE LATER, AND WHICH ARE NOT
-----------------------------------------------------
  recall_strict   comparable v1 -> v2. This is the migration metric.
  recall (plain)  NOT comparable v1 -> v2. v1 enjoys a packed-ref subsidy
                  (one chunk reffed cl. 16,17 scoring two expected refs)
                  that corpus v2 removes on purpose. On frozen-10 that
                  subsidy is worth 0.925 - 0.701 = 0.224.
  recall@k curve  comparable within a corpus version only; the pool is
                  k=20/doc, which is NOT the shipping arm.
  context precision 0.333 / reverse_rel 0.630  function of chunk count and
                  top_n; both are retired as descriptions of the system in
                  Phase F, though their REASONING survives.
