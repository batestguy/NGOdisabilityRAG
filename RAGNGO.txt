Project Request: Disability Rights Legal & Connection Assistant
📌 Project Overview
Project Name: Disability Rights Legal & Connection Assistant (DRLCA)

Request Type: Flexible RAG-based conversational AI system for Nigerian disability rights advocacy

Goal: Build a tool that helps Nigerians with disabilities understand their legal rights and connect with verified support organizations—using only free cloud platforms and open-source tools.

🎯 Core Objectives
Primary Goal
Create an accessible, intelligent assistant that empowers persons with disabilities (PWDs) in Nigeria by:

Translating complex legal language from the Disability Act 2018 into plain, understandable answers.

Connecting users to relevant NGOs and helplines based on their specific disability type and location.

Adapting to user needs through accessibility-first design (voice, screen readers, plain language).

Secondary Goal
Build a flexible, research-friendly system that allows for continuous learning and adaptation as new frameworks and techniques emerge.

📋 Core Features
Feature 1: Legal Q&A RAG
Users ask questions about their rights in plain English, Pidgin, or Nigerian languages.

System retrieves relevant sections from the Disability Act and Constitution.

Every answer must include direct citations (e.g., "Section 17(2) of the Disability Act states...").

Plain language toggle for users with cognitive disabilities or low literacy.

Feature 2: NGO & Helpline Connector
Users state their disability type and location.

System returns a curated list of relevant organizations with contact details.

National helplines always displayed at the top.

Results should be filterable and sortable.

Feature 3: Intent Router
System automatically detects whether user needs legal information or NGO connections.

Routes queries to the appropriate subsystem.

Feature 4: Accessibility-First Interface
Voice input/output support.

Screen reader compatibility.

High contrast and font scaling options.

Keyboard navigable.

Simple, clean UI appropriate for users with varying digital literacy.
